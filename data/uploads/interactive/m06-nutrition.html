🔄 The Cycle That RepeatsMzunguko UnaojirudiaCycle Inayojirudia

Nutrition affects every stage of life, and poor nutrition at any stage disrupts the ones that follow — including the next generation.Lishe huathiri kila hatua ya maisha, na lishe duni katika hatua yoyote huvuruga zinazofuata — ikiwemo kizazi kijacho.Nutrition inaathiri kila stage ya maisha, na poor nutrition kwa stage yoyote ina-disrupt zinazofuata — ikiwemo generation ijayo.

⚠️ The intergenerational cycle of undernutrition⚠️ Mzunguko wa vizazi wa utapiamlo⚠️ Intergenerational cycle ya undernutrition A girl who is stunted is more likely to give birth to a low-birth-weight baby. If that baby is a girl, and nothing intervenes, the cycle continues into the generation after. This is why adolescent girls' nutrition is treated as a public health priority and not a personal preference. Msichana aliyedumaa ana uwezekano mkubwa wa kuzaa mtoto mwenye uzito mdogo. Mtoto huyo akiwa msichana, na hakuna kinachoingilia, mzunguko huendelea hadi kizazi kinachofuata. Ndiyo maana lishe ya wasichana vijana huchukuliwa kama kipaumbele cha afya ya umma na si upendeleo binafsi. Dem aliye-stunted ana uwezekano mkubwa wa kuzaa mtoto mwenye low birth weight. Huyo mtoto akiwa dem, na hakuna kinacho-intervene, cycle inaendelea hadi generation inayofuata. Ndio maana nutrition ya wasichana vijana ina-treatiwa kama public health priority si personal preference.
