  • Difficulty breathing, speaking or coughingUgumu wa kupumua, kuzungumza au kukohoaUgumu wa kupumua, kuongea ama kukohoa

⚠️ Someone choking may make no sound at all⚠️ Anayekwama anaweza asitoe sauti kabisa⚠️ Msee anaye-choke anaweza asitoe sauti kabisa A person coughing forcefully still has an airway. A person who cannot cough, speak or breathe is the emergency — and they will be silent. Hands at the throat is the universal signal. Do not wait for noise. Mtu anayekohoa kwa nguvu bado ana njia ya hewa. Mtu asiyeweza kukohoa, kuzungumza wala kupumua ndiye dharura — na atakuwa kimya. Mikono kooni ndiyo ishara ya kimataifa. Usisubiri kelele. Msee anayekohoa kwa nguvu bado ana airway. Msee asiyeweza kukohoa, kuongea wala kupumua ndio emergency — na atakuwa kimya. Mikono kooni ndio universal signal. Usingoje kelele.

💡 The answer to the Big Question💡 Jibu la Swali Kubwa💡 Jibu la Big Question Almost every item on that list is visible before the accident, not after. What people fail to see is not the danger itself but the ordinariness of it — the missing helmet on a route ridden a hundred times, the unlit stairs used every night. Predictable is not the same as noticed. Karibu kila kitu kwenye orodha hiyo kinaonekana kabla ya ajali, si baadaye. Watu hushindwa kuona si hatari yenyewe bali ukawaida wake — kofia inayokosekana kwenye njia iliyopitiwa mara mia, ngazi zisizo na mwanga zinazotumika kila usiku. Kutabirika si sawa na kuonwa. Karibu kila kitu kwa hiyo list kinaonekana kabla ya accident, si baadaye. Wasee wanashindwa kuona si danger yenyewe ni ordinariness yake — helmet inayokosekana kwa route iliyo-ridiwa mara mia, unlit stairs zinazotumika kila usiku. Predictable si sawa na noticed.

  2. Call for help immediately and say clearly where you are, what happened, and how many people are hurt.Piga simu ya msaada mara moja na useme wazi ulipo, kilichotokea, na watu wangapi wamejeruhiwa.Call for help immediately na useme wazi uko wapi, kilicho-happen, na wasee wangapi wame-injuriwa.
