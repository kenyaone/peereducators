💡 The answer to the Big Question💡 Jibu la Swali Kubwa💡 Jibu la Big Question Teach both columns together, never one alone. A peer educator who only lists dangers gets ignored by people who use these tools happily every day; one who only celebrates the benefits leaves them defenceless. Digital platforms can help or harm depending on how they are used — that is the whole message, and it is why digital literacy is a skill rather than a warning. Fundisha safu zote mbili pamoja, kamwe si moja peke yake. Mwelimishaji rika anayeorodhesha hatari tu hupuuzwa na wanaotumia zana hizi kwa furaha kila siku; anayesifia faida tu huwaacha bila ulinzi. Majukwaa ya kidijitali yanaweza kusaidia au kudhuru kutegemea yanavyotumika — huo ndio ujumbe wote, na ndiyo maana ujuzi wa kidijitali ni stadi si onyo. Fundisha columns zote mbili pamoja, kamwe si moja peke yake. Peer educator anaye-list dangers tu ana-ignoriwa na wasee wanaotumia hizi tools happily kila siku; anaye-celebrate benefits tu anawaacha bila protection. Digital platforms zinaweza kusaidia ama kudhuru kutegemea zinavyotumika — huo ndio message wote, na ndio maana digital literacy ni skill si warning.
