📖 What Rights Actually AreHaki ni Nini HasaRights ni Nini Hasa
🔑 Adolescent rights🔑 Haki za vijana🔑 Adolescent rightsThe legal, social and ethical entitlements that protect and empower adolescents aged 10 to 19. They help young people grow, participate and thrive, while safeguarding them from harm.Stahili za kisheria, kijamii na kimaadili zinazolinda na kuwawezesha vijana wa miaka 10 hadi 19. Husaidia vijana kukua, kushiriki na kustawi, huku wakilindwa dhidi ya madhara.Legal, social na ethical entitlements zinazolinda na ku-empower vijana wa miaka 10 hadi 19. Zinasaidia vijana kukua, ku-participate na ku-thrive, huku wakilindwa dhidi ya harm.

They are entitlements guaranteed by law — not favours granted by adults, and not privileges that can be withdrawn for bad behaviour. They rest on three principles: equality, dignity, and the best interests of the child.Ni stahili zinazohakikishwa na sheria — si upendeleo unaotolewa na watu wazima, wala si vibali vinavyoweza kuondolewa kwa tabia mbaya. Zinasimama kwenye kanuni tatu: usawa, hadhi, na maslahi bora ya mtoto.Ni entitlements zinazo-guaranteewa na sheria — si favours zinazotolewa na watu wazima, wala si privileges zinazoweza ku-withdrawiwa kwa bad behaviour. Zinasimama kwa principles tatu: equality, dignity, na best interests za mtoto.

⚠️ The distinction to hold onto⚠️ Tofauti ya kushikilia⚠️ Tofauti ya kushikilia A privilege is something you earn and can lose. A right is something you have because you exist. If an adult ever frames a young person's health care, education or safety as something they must deserve, they have described a privilege — and that is not what the law says. Kibali ni kitu unachokipata na unaweza kukipoteza. Haki ni kitu unacho kwa sababu upo. Mtu mzima akiwahi kueleza huduma ya afya, elimu au usalama wa kijana kama kitu anachopaswa kustahili, ameeleza kibali — na si hivyo sheria inavyosema. Privilege ni kitu unachoi-earn na unaweza kuipoteza. Right ni kitu ulicho nacho kwa sababu upo. Mtu mzima akiwahi ku-frame health care, education ama safety ya kijana kama kitu anachofaa ku-deserve, ame-describe privilege — na si hivyo sheria inavyosema.

💡 Why the African Charter matters in this conversation💡 Kwa nini Mkataba wa Afrika ni muhimu hapa💡 Kwa nini African Charter ni muhimu hapa When someone defends FGM or child marriage as African tradition, the strongest answer is not a Western treaty — it is that African states wrote their own charter, in their own cultural context, and prohibited those practices themselves. That is a much harder argument to dismiss as foreign. Mtu anapotetea ukeketaji au ndoa za utotoni kama mila ya Kiafrika, jibu lenye nguvu zaidi si mkataba wa Magharibi — ni kwamba mataifa ya Afrika yaliandika mkataba wao wenyewe, katika muktadha wao wa kitamaduni, na yakakataza mila hizo wenyewe. Hiyo ni hoja ngumu zaidi kuipuuza kama ya kigeni. Msee anapo-defend FGM ama child marriage kama African tradition, answer yenye nguvu zaidi si Western treaty — ni ati African states ziliandika charter yao wenyewe, kwa cultural context yao, na zika-prohibit hizo practices wenyewe. Hiyo ni argument ngumu zaidi kui-dismiss kama ya kigeni.

🔑 Key MessagesUjumbe MuhimuKey Messages
  • Adolescent rights are enshrined in the Kenyan Constitution, national policy, and regional and international treaties Kenya has signed.Haki za vijana zimo katika Katiba ya Kenya, sera za kitaifa, na mikataba ya kikanda na kimataifa Kenya imetia saini.Adolescent rights ziko kwa Constitution ya Kenya, national policy, na regional na international treaties Kenya ime-sign.
  • Adolescents have rights to health, education, information, and privacy and confidentiality.Vijana wana haki za afya, elimu, taarifa, na faragha na usiri.Vijana wana rights za health, education, information, na privacy na confidentiality.
  • Rights, information and freedoms should be exercised responsibly.Haki, taarifa na uhuru vinapaswa kutumiwa kwa uwajibikaji.Rights, information na freedoms zinafaa kutumiwa responsibly.
  • Child marriage, FGM and hazardous work are violations, not traditions.Ndoa za utotoni, ukeketaji na kazi hatari ni ukiukaji, si mila.Child marriage, FGM na hazardous work ni violations, si traditions.
  • Knowing where to report a violation is part of knowing your rights.Kujua wapi kuripoti ukiukaji ni sehemu ya kujua haki zako.Kujua wapi ku-report violation ni part ya kujua rights zako.
