🔑 Peer🔑 Rika🔑 PeerA person who belongs to the same social group as another — similar age, background or experience.Mtu anayetoka kundi moja la kijamii na mwingine — umri, asili au uzoefu unaofanana.Msee wa group moja na wewe — same age, background ama experience.
🔑 Education🔑 Elimu🔑 EducationThe development of a person's knowledge, attitudes, beliefs or behaviour as a result of learning.Ukuzaji wa maarifa, mitazamo, imani au tabia ya mtu kutokana na kujifunza.Ku-develop knowledge, attitude, imani ama behaviour ya msee kutokana na kujifunza.
🔑 Peer Education🔑 Elimu ya Rika🔑 Peer EducationThe transfer of knowledge and skills to members of a social group by others within that same group.Uhamishaji wa maarifa na ujuzi kwa wanachama wa kundi la kijamii na wengine ndani ya kundi hilo hilo.Ku-transfer knowledge na skills kwa wasee wa group yako na wasee wengine wa group hiyo hiyo.
🔑 Peer Educator🔑 Mwelimishaji Rika🔑 Peer EducatorA trained young person who gives information, guidance and support on health, rights and life skills to their peers in a way those peers can relate to.Kijana aliyefunzwa anayetoa taarifa, mwongozo na msaada kuhusu afya, haki na ujuzi wa maisha kwa wenzake kwa njia wanayoweza kuielewa.Kijana aliye-trained anayetoa info, guidance na support kuhusu health, rights na life skills kwa wasee wenzake kwa njia wanaweza ku-relate.
💡 Remember💡 Kumbuka💡 Remember Peer educators are usually volunteers. You do this because you chose to, which is exactly why your peers trust it. Waelimishaji rika kwa kawaida ni wa kujitolea. Unafanya hivi kwa sababu ulichagua, na ndiyo maana wenzako wanaamini. Peer educators ni volunteers mostly. Unafanya hii kwa sababu ulichagua — na ndio maana wasee wanatrust.

⭐ Qualities of a Peer EducatorSifa za Mwelimishaji RikaQualities za Peer Educator
  • Confident and friendly — approachable, with a positive attitudeMwenye kujiamini na urafiki — anayefikika, mwenye mtazamo chanyaConfident na friendly — approachable, na attitude poa
  • Active and respected — trusted by peers and committed to the roleMwenye bidii na anayeheshimiwa — anayeaminika na wenzake na aliyejitoleaActive na respected — anatrustiwa na wasee na amecommit kwa role
  • Good listener and communicator — clear, engaging and empatheticMsikilizaji na mwasilianaji mzuri — wazi, mvutiaji na mwenye hurumaGood listener na communicator — clear, engaging na empathetic
  • Non-judgemental and open-minded — creates a safe, inclusive spaceAsiyehukumu na mwenye akili wazi — huunda nafasi salama, jumuishiNon-judgemental na open-minded — anacreate safe space kwa kila mtu
  • A leader peers relate to — natural leader who speaks their languageKiongozi wenzake wanaomwelewa — kiongozi wa asili anayezungumza lugha yaoLeader ambaye wasee wanamrelate — natural leader anaye-speak lugha yao
  • Confidential — trustworthy with sensitive informationMwenye siri — anayeaminika na taarifa nyetiConfidential — anatrustiwa na sensitive info
⚠️ The one that ends your work⚠️ Ile inayomaliza kazi yako⚠️ Ile inayoisha job yako Break confidentiality once and it is over. Word travels faster than you do, and no peer will bring you anything real again. Confidentiality is not a nice quality — it is the licence to operate. Vunja usiri mara moja na imekwisha. Habari husafiri haraka kuliko wewe, na hakuna rika atakayekuletea jambo la kweli tena. Usiri si sifa nzuri tu — ni leseni ya kufanya kazi. Vunja confidentiality mara moja na imeisha. Story inatravel faster kuliko wewe, na hakuna msee atakuletea kitu real tena. Confidentiality si quality nzuri tu — ni licence ya kufanya hii job.

🚧 Know Where Your Role EndsJua Jukumu Lako LinapoishiaJua Role Yako Inaishia Wapi

The most damaging mistake a peer educator makes is not giving wrong information. It is trying to handle something that should have been referred.Kosa linalodhuru zaidi ambalo mwelimishaji rika hufanya si kutoa taarifa isiyo sahihi. Ni kujaribu kushughulikia jambo lililopaswa kupelekwa kwa mtaalamu.Mistake mbaya zaidi ambayo peer educator anafanya si kutoa info mbaya. Ni ku-try kuhandle kitu ambacho kilifaa ku-referiwa.

  • You are not a counsellor, a clinician or a police officerWewe si mshauri, daktari au afisa wa polisiWewe si counsellor, clinician ama polisi
  • You do not diagnose, prescribe, or promise an outcomeHutambui ugonjwa, huagizi dawa, wala huahidi matokeoHu-diagnose, hu-prescribe, wala huahidi outcome
  • You refer, and you follow up that the referral landedUnatoa rufaa, na unafuatilia kwamba rufaa ilifikaUna-refer, na una-follow up kama referral ilifika
  • You never keep a disclosure of abuse or suicide to yourselfHutunzi siri ya ufichuzi wa unyanyasaji au kujiuaHuwezi keep disclosure ya abuse ama suicide kwako
⚠️ Escalate immediately⚠️ Peleka juu mara moja⚠️ Escalate immediately Any mention of suicide, sexual abuse, or violence at home goes to your supervising adult the same day. Confidentiality protects private matters — it does not cover situations where someone is in danger. Kutajwa kwa kujiua, unyanyasaji wa kingono, au vurugu nyumbani kunaenda kwa mtu mzima anayekusimamia siku hiyo hiyo. Usiri hulinda mambo binafsi — haufuniki hali ambapo mtu yuko hatarini. Kutajwa kwa suicide, sexual abuse, ama violence nyumbani inaenda kwa msimamizi wako siku hiyo hiyo. Confidentiality inalinda private matters — haicovers situation ambapo mtu yuko hatarini.

🔑 Key MessagesUjumbe MuhimuKey Messages
  • Peer education builds trust, empowers peers and reaches hard-to-reach groups.Elimu ya rika hujenga imani, huwawezesha wenzako na hufikia makundi magumu kufikiwa.Peer education inajenga trust, ina-empower wasee na inareach groups ngumu kureach.
  • Peer educators are role models who share accurate information and link peers to services.Waelimishaji rika ni mifano ya kuigwa wanaotoa taarifa sahihi na kuwaunganisha wenzao na huduma.Peer educators ni role models wanaoshare info sahihi na ku-link wasee na services.
  • Effective peer educators are confident, approachable, non-judgmental and keep confidentiality.Waelimishaji rika bora wanajiamini, wanafikika, hawahukumu na wanatunza siri.Peer educators poa ni confident, approachable, non-judgmental na wanakeep confidentiality.
  • Knowing where your role ends is as important as knowing what it covers.Kujua jukumu lako linapoishia ni muhimu kama kujua linahusu nini.Kujua role yako inaishia wapi ni muhimu kama kujua inacover nini.
