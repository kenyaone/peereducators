🤲 Running an Inclusive SessionKuendesha Kipindi JumuishiKu-run Inclusive Session

An inclusive session is one where the least confident person present still takes part. That is the test — not how much fun the most athletic people had.Kipindi jumuishi ni kile ambapo mtu asiye na ujasiri zaidi aliyepo bado hushiriki. Hicho ndicho kipimo — si furaha kiasi gani watu wenye siha zaidi walipata.Inclusive session ni ile ambapo msee asiye na confidence zaidi aliyepo bado ana-participate. Hicho ndio test — si fun kiasi gani wasee wenye fitness zaidi walipata.

  • Offer levels, not one standard. Walk, jog or run — same activity, three entry points.Toa viwango, si kiwango kimoja. Tembea, kimbia polepole au kimbia — shughuli ile ile, njia tatu za kuingia.Toa levels, si standard moja. Tembea, jog ama run — activity ile ile, entry points tatu.
  • Avoid picking teams by captains. It publicly ranks everyone, and the same people are chosen last every time.Epuka kuchagua timu kwa manahodha. Hupanga kila mtu hadharani, na watu wale wale huchaguliwa mwisho kila mara.Avoid ku-pick teams na captains. Ina-rank kila msee publicly, na wasee wale wale wana-choosiwa last kila time.
  • Adapt rather than exclude. Someone who cannot run can keep score, throw, or play a seated version.Rekebisha badala ya kuwatenga. Asiyeweza kukimbia anaweza kuhesabu alama, kurusha, au kucheza toleo la kukaa.Adapt badala ya ku-exclude. Asiyeweza kukimbia anaweza ku-keep score, ku-throw, ama kucheza seated version.
  • Ask about health conditions quietly before you start — asthma, sickle cell, an old injury. Never in front of the group.Uliza kuhusu hali za afya kwa utulivu kabla ya kuanza — pumu, selimundu, jeraha la zamani. Kamwe si mbele ya kundi.Uliza kuhusu health conditions kwa utulivu kabla uanze — asthma, sickle cell, injury ya zamani. Kamwe si mbele ya group.
  • Make it free. If a session needs shoes, gear or fees, it excludes exactly the peers who benefit most.Ifanye bure. Kipindi kikihitaji viatu, vifaa au ada, kinawatenga hasa wenzako wanaonufaika zaidi.Ifanye free. Session ikihitaji viatu, gear ama fees, ina-exclude hasa wasee wanao-benefit zaidi.

🔑 Key MessagesUjumbe MuhimuKey Messages
  • Physical activity is a tool for social connection, not just fitness.Mazoezi ni chombo cha kuunganisha kijamii, si siha tu.Physical activity ni tool ya social connection, si fitness tu.
  • Any activity is better than none — all movement counts.Shughuli yoyote ni bora kuliko hakuna — kila mwendo unahesabika.Activity yoyote ni better kuliko hakuna — kila movement ina-count.
  • Design sessions that prioritise fun, interaction and emotional well-being alongside physical health.Buni vipindi vinavyopa kipaumbele furaha, mwingiliano na ustawi wa kihisia pamoja na afya ya mwili.Design sessions zinazo-prioritise fun, interaction na emotional well-being pamoja na physical health.
  • Use games and group challenges to break down barriers and build trust.Tumia michezo na changamoto za kikundi kuvunja vizuizi na kujenga imani.Tumia games na group challenges ku-break barriers na kujenga trust.
  • Shared movement reduces isolation and builds mental resilience.Mwendo wa pamoja hupunguza upweke na kujenga uthabiti wa akili.Shared movement inapunguza isolation na kujenga mental resilience.
