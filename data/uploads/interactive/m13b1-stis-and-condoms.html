🛡️ Condoms and Dual ProtectionKondomu na Ulinzi MaradufuCondoms na Dual Protection

A condom is a barrier method — worn on the penis (male condom, latex or non-latex for those with a latex allergy) or inserted into the vagina (female condom, made of plastic).Kondomu ni njia ya kizuizi — huvaliwa kwenye uume (kondomu ya kiume, mpira au isiyo ya mpira kwa wenye mzio) au huingizwa ukeni (kondomu ya kike, iliyotengenezwa kwa plastiki).Condom ni barrier method — inavaliwa kwa penis (male condom, latex ama non-latex kwa wenye latex allergy) ama inaingizwa kwa vagina (female condom, ya plastic).

🔑 Dual Protection🔑 Ulinzi Maradufu🔑 Dual ProtectionCondoms protect against infections AND pregnancy at the same time. That makes them unique among contraceptives — no other method does both.Kondomu hulinda dhidi ya maambukizi NA ujauzito kwa wakati mmoja. Hilo huzifanya za kipekee kati ya njia za uzazi wa mpango — hakuna njia nyingine inayofanya vyote viwili.Condoms zinalinda dhidi ya infections NA pregnancy kwa wakati mmoja. Hiyo inazifanya unique kati ya contraceptives — hakuna method nyingine inayofanya zote mbili.

  • Prevent unintended pregnanciesHuzuia mimba zisizotarajiwaZina-prevent unintended pregnancies

⚠️ Correct AND consistent⚠️ Sahihi NA kila mara⚠️ Correct NA consistent Effectiveness drops sharply with incorrect or inconsistent use. 'Most of the time' is not how condoms work — one unprotected occasion is enough. Correct use every single time is the whole message. Ufanisi hushuka sana kwa matumizi yasiyo sahihi au yasiyo ya kila mara. 'Mara nyingi' si jinsi kondomu zinavyofanya kazi — tukio moja bila kinga linatosha. Matumizi sahihi kila mara ndio ujumbe wote. Effectiveness inashuka sana na incorrect ama inconsistent use. 'Most of the time' si jinsi condoms zinavyowork — occasion moja bila protection inatosha. Correct use kila time ndio message yote.

🔑 Key MessagesUjumbe MuhimuKey Messages
  • STIs spread through sexual contact — not by hugging or sharing food.Magonjwa ya zinaa huenea kwa kugusana kingono — si kwa kukumbatiana au kushiriki chakula.STIs zinaspread kwa sexual contact — si kwa kukumbatiana ama kushare chakula.

  • Many STIs are silent — regular testing, and testing before a new relationship, is how they are found.Magonjwa mengi ya zinaa ni kimya — kupimwa mara kwa mara, na kabla ya uhusiano mpya, ndiko kunakoyagundua.STIs nyingi ni silent — regular testing, na kupimwa kabla ya relationship mpya, ndio inazigundua.
  • Most STIs are curable. Complete the treatment, inform partners, and follow up.Magonjwa mengi ya zinaa yanatibika. Maliza matibabu, wajulishe wenzi, na fuatilia.STIs nyingi zina-cure. Maliza treatment, inform partners, na follow up.
  • Condoms are the only method that prevents both infection and pregnancy — but only with correct, consistent use.Kondomu ndiyo njia pekee inayozuia maambukizi na ujauzito — lakini kwa matumizi sahihi ya kila mara tu.Condoms ndio method pekee inayozuia infection na pregnancy — lakini na correct, consistent use tu.
