📖 The Words That Get ConfusedManeno YanayochanganywaManeno Yanayo-confusiwa

'Use', 'abuse', 'dependence' and 'addiction' get used interchangeably in ordinary conversation. They are not the same, and mixing them up is how a real problem gets dismissed as ordinary behaviour.'Matumizi', 'matumizi mabaya', 'utegemezi' na 'uraibu' hutumiwa kwa kubadilishana katika mazungumzo ya kawaida. Si sawa, na kuyachanganya ndiko kunakofanya tatizo halisi lipuuzwe kama tabia ya kawaida.'Use', 'abuse', 'dependence' na 'addiction' zinatumiwa interchangeably kwa ordinary conversation. Si sawa, na kuzi-mix ndio inafanya real problem i-dismissiwe kama ordinary behaviour.

🔑 Key MessagesUjumbe MuhimuKey Messages
  • Use, abuse, dependence and addiction are different things — precision matters.Matumizi, matumizi mabaya, utegemezi na uraibu ni vitu tofauti — usahihi ni muhimu.Use, abuse, dependence na addiction ni vitu tofauti — precision ni muhimu.
  • Legal does not mean safe. The most damaging substances among Kenyan adolescents are largely the legal ones.Halali si salama. Vitu vinavyodhuru zaidi kwa vijana wa Kenya ni vile halali kwa wingi.Legal si safe. Substances zinazodhuru zaidi kwa vijana wa Kenya ni zile legal mostly.
  • Dependence is a slope of five stages, not a single event.Utegemezi ni mteremko wa hatua tano, si tukio moja.Dependence ni slope ya stages tano, si event moja.
  • Almost every risk factor is something that happened to a young person, not something they chose.Karibu kila sababu ya hatari ni jambo lililomtokea kijana, si alilochagua.Karibu kila risk factor ni kitu kilicho-happen kwa kijana, si alichochagua.
