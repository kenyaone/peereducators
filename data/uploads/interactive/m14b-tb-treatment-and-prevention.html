⚠️ The answer to the Big Question⚠️ Jibu la Swali Kubwa⚠️ Jibu la Big Question Stopping the medicine early because you feel better. TB medicine makes a person feel well long before the bacteria are gone. Someone who stops at that point is not cured — they relapse, and the germs that survived are the drug-resistant ones. Never stop TB medicine without a doctor's advice, even if you feel completely fine. Kuacha dawa mapema kwa sababu unajisikia vizuri. Dawa ya TB humfanya mtu ajisikie vizuri muda mrefu kabla bakteria hawajaisha. Anayeacha wakati huo hajapona — hurudiwa, na vijidudu vilivyonusurika ndivyo sugu kwa dawa. Usiache kamwe dawa ya TB bila ushauri wa daktari, hata ukijisikia vizuri kabisa. Ku-stop dawa mapema kwa sababu unajiskia better. TB medicine inamfanya msee ajiskie poa muda mrefu kabla bacteria ziishe. Msee anaye-stop wakati huo haja-cure — ana-relapse, na germs zilizo-survive ndio drug-resistant. Usi-stop kamwe TB medicine bila advice ya daktari, hata ukijiskia poa kabisa.

⚠️ Contacts are not only family⚠️ Waliokutana si familia tu⚠️ Contacts si family tu The curriculum is explicit that contacts also include the people a young person spends most of their time with — at school and in the community. For an adolescent, that is a classroom and a dormitory, not just a home. If a classmate is diagnosed, the class is a contact group, and saying so is not gossip — it is how transmission stops. Mtaala uko wazi kwamba waliokutana pia ni pamoja na watu ambao kijana hutumia muda mwingi nao — shuleni na katika jamii. Kwa kijana, hilo ni darasa na bweni, si nyumba tu. 🎓 Facilitator note · 10 min
Do the ventilation audit with the group in the room you are sitting in. How many windows are there, how many are open, and who decided that? In most Kenyan classrooms the answer is that they are shut because of noise or cold, and nobody has ever weighed that against TB. It is the one prevention step the group can change today, themselves, for free.Fanya ukaguzi wa uingizaji hewa na kundi katika chumba mnamokaa. Kuna madirisha mangapi, mangapi yamefunguliwa, na nani aliamua hivyo? Katika madarasa mengi ya Kenya jibu ni kwamba yamefungwa kwa sababu ya kelele au baridi, na hakuna aliyewahi kupima hilo dhidi ya kifua kikuu. Ni hatua moja ya kinga ambayo kundi linaweza kuibadilisha leo, wenyewe, bure.Fanya ventilation audit na group kwa room mnamokaa. Kuna windows ngapi, ngapi zimefunguliwa, na nani ali-decide hivyo? Kwa classrooms nyingi za Kenya answer ni ati zimefungwa kwa sababu ya kelele ama baridi, na hakuna msee amewahi ku-weigh hiyo dhidi ya TB. Ni prevention step moja ambayo group inaweza kui-change leo, wenyewe, free.

Those are Kenya's national and the global TB elimination goals. Notice that the third one — zero TB suffering — is not about the bacteria at all. It is about how people with TB are treated by everyone around them.Hayo ni malengo ya kitaifa ya Kenya na ya kimataifa ya kutokomeza kifua kikuu. Angalia kwamba la tatu — sifuri mateso ya TB — halihusu bakteria kabisa. Linahusu jinsi watu wenye TB wanavyotendewa na wote waliowazunguka.Hayo ni national goals za Kenya na global TB elimination goals. Angalia ati la tatu — zero TB suffering — halihusu bacteria kabisa. ⚠️ The part that belongs to you⚠️ Sehemu inayokuhusu wewe⚠️ Part inayokuhusu wewe You cannot deliver zero deaths or zero disease on your own — those need clinics, drugs and systems. Zero suffering is largely stigma, and stigma is made of what people say in classrooms and at home. That one is genuinely within your reach, and it starts with correcting the airborne-not-contact fact every single time you hear it wrong. Huwezi kufikisha sifuri vifo au sifuri ugonjwa peke yako — hayo yanahitaji kliniki, dawa na mifumo. Sifuri mateso ni unyanyapaa kwa kiasi kikubwa, na unyanyapaa umeundwa na yale watu husema madarasani na nyumbani. Hilo liko ndani ya uwezo wako kweli, na huanza kwa kusahihisha ukweli wa hewa-si-kugusana kila mara unaposikia vibaya. Huwezi ku-deliver zero deaths ama zero disease peke yako — hizo zinahitaji clinics, dawa na systems. Zero suffering ni stigma kwa kiasi kikubwa, na stigma ime-madiwa na yale wasee wanasema classrooms na nyumbani. Hiyo iko ndani ya reach yako kweli, na inaanza kwa ku-correct fact ya airborne-si-contact kila time unasikia vibaya.
