Suicide is when someone ends their own life. It is often the result of feeling overwhelmed, hopeless, or believing there is no other way out. That last part matters most: what the person needs is not to be told they are wrong, but to be shown another way out exists.Kujiua ni pale mtu anapoyamaliza maisha yake. 💡 The answer to the Big Question💡 Jibu la Swali Kubwa💡 Jibu la Big Question Asking someone directly whether they are thinking about suicide does not put the idea there. For someone already carrying it, being asked is usually the first relief they have had — because it means somebody noticed, and somebody can hear the answer. Ask plainly: 'Are you thinking about ending your life?' Kumuuliza mtu moja kwa moja kama anafikiria kujiua hakumtii wazo hilo. Kwa mtu ambaye tayari analibeba, kuulizwa mara nyingi ndiyo faraja ya kwanza aliyopata — kwa sababu inamaanisha mtu aliona, na mtu anaweza kusikia jibu. Uliza wazi: 'Je, unafikiria kuyamaliza maisha yako?' Kumuuliza msee directly kama anafikiria suicide haimtii hilo wazo. Kwa msee ambaye tayari analibeba, kuulizwa mara nyingi ndio relief ya kwanza amepata — kwa sababu inamaanisha msee aliona, na msee anaweza kusikia jibu. Uliza wazi: 'Je, unafikiria ku-end maisha yako?'
  1. Listen without judgement. Do not argue, do not minimise, do not rush to fix it.Sikiliza bila kuhukumu. Usibishane, usidharau, usikimbilie kurekebisha.Listen bila kujudge. Usi-argue, usi-minimise, usikimbilie ku-fix.
  2. Seek help from an adult — a parent, guardian, community health promoter, church elder or mental health professional.Tafuta msaada kwa mtu mzima — mzazi, mlezi, mhudumu wa afya wa jamii, mzee wa kanisa au mtaalamu wa afya ya akili.Tafuta msaada kwa mtu mzima — mzazi, guardian, community health promoter, church elder ama mental health professional.
  3. Encourage professional help — and offer to go with them. The offer to go is usually what makes it happen.Himiza msaada wa kitaalamu — na jitolee kwenda naye. Kujitolea kwenda ndiko kwa kawaida kunakofanya litokee.Encourage professional help — na offer kwenda naye. Hiyo offer ya kwenda ndio kwa kawaida inafanya i-happen.
  4. Remove things that could be used — knives, pesticides and similar items should not be within reach.Ondoa vitu vinavyoweza kutumika — visu, dawa za wadudu na vitu kama hivyo visiwe karibu.Ondoa vitu vinavyoweza kutumika — visu, pesticides na vitu kama hivyo visiwe karibu.
  5. Stay connected. Keep checking on them regularly — not once, and not only in a crisis.Endelea kuwasiliana. Endelea kumwangalia mara kwa mara — si mara moja, na si tu wakati wa dharura.Stay connected. Endelea kum-check regularly — si mara moja, na si tu wakati wa crisis.

🎲 Betting and Mental HealthKamari na Afya ya AkiliBetting na Mental Health

Betting is not a side issue in Kenya — it is among the most live mental health risks facing young men in particular. The curriculum treats it as a mental health topic, and so should you.Kamari si suala la pembeni Kenya — ni kati ya hatari kubwa zaidi za afya ya akili zinazowakabili vijana wa kiume hasa. Mtaala unaichukulia kama mada ya afya ya akili, na wewe pia unapaswa.Betting si side issue Kenya — ni kati ya mental health risks live zaidi zinazowakabili vijana wa kiume hasa. Curriculum inai-treat kama mental health topic, na wewe pia unafaa.

  1. The systems are designed to keep you hooked — they work directly on the brain's reward system.Mifumo imeundwa kukuweka umenaswa — hufanya kazi moja kwa moja kwenye mfumo wa zawadi wa ubongo.Systems zime-designiwa kuku-keep hooked — zinafanya kazi directly kwa reward system ya brain.
  2. Occasional wins create a false sense of control — you remember the wins and forget the losses, which is exactly what the design intends.Ushindi wa mara chache huunda hisia potofu ya udhibiti — unakumbuka ushindi na kusahau hasara, ambayo ndiyo hasa muundo unakusudia.Occasional wins zina-create false sense ya control — unakumbuka wins na kusahau losses, ambayo ndio hasa design inakusudia.
  3. Anxiety and depression follow — both from the losses and from hiding them.Wasiwasi na msongo wa mawazo hufuata — kutokana na hasara na kutokana na kuzificha.Anxiety na depression zina-follow — kutokana na losses na kutokana na kuzificha.
  4. Financial stress worsens mental health, which drives more betting to recover the loss. That is the loop.Msongo wa kifedha huzidisha afya mbaya ya akili, ambayo husukuma kamari zaidi kurudisha hasara. Huo ndio mzunguko.Financial stress inazidisha mental health mbaya, ambayo ina-drive betting zaidi ku-recover loss. Hiyo ndio loop.
  5. When betting starts controlling you, it is no longer entertainment. That is the sentence to give a peer.Kamari inapoanza kukudhibiti, si burudani tena. Hiyo ndiyo sentensi ya kumpa rika.Betting inapoanza kuku-control, si entertainment tena. Hiyo ndio sentence ya kumpa msee.

  • Social support — talking with friends, family or a support group reduces isolation and shares the loadMsaada wa kijamii — kuzungumza na marafiki, familia au kikundi cha msaada hupunguza upweke na kugawana mzigoSocial support — kuongea na friends, family ama support group inapunguza isolation na ku-share load

  • Medication when prescribed — antidepressants, mood stabilisers or antipsychotics, under medical supervision onlyDawa zikiagizwa — dawa za msongo, za kutuliza hisia au za akili, chini ya usimamizi wa daktari pekeeMedication ikiwa prescribed — antidepressants, mood stabilisers ama antipsychotics, chini ya medical supervision pekee

Kama ilivyo kwa PrEP katika Moduli 13B: kutaja kwamba dawa zipo ni kazi yako. Kuamua kama mtu anazihitaji, ipi, au kama aache kuzitumia, si kazi yako. Kuacha dawa za akili ghafla kunaweza kuwa hatari — daima peleka swali hilo kwa daktari aliyeagiza. Kama PrEP kwa Module 13B: ku-name ati medication iko ni job yako. Ku-decide kama msee anaihitaji, ipi, ama kama aache kuitumia, si job yako. Kuacha psychiatric medication ghafla inaweza kuwa hatari — daima route hiyo question kwa prescriber.
