💡 The distinction that matters💡 Tofauti ya maana💡 Tofauti ya maana SBCC targets the community. Advocacy targets the people who decide. Persuading a hundred young people to seek contraception achieves little if the clinic has none in stock — that second problem is fixed by advocacy, not by another session. Knowing which of the two a problem needs is the whole skill of this module. SBCC hulenga jamii. Utetezi hulenga wanaoamua. Kuwashawishi vijana mia moja watafute uzazi wa mpango hakusaidii sana kama kliniki haina bidhaa — tatizo hilo la pili hurekebishwa kwa utetezi, si kwa kipindi kingine. Kujua ni lipi kati ya mawili tatizo linahitaji ndio ujuzi wote wa moduli hii. SBCC ina-target community. Advocacy ina-target wanao-decide. Ku-persuade vijana mia moja watafute contraception haisaidii sana kama clinic haina stock — hiyo problem ya pili ina-fixiwa na advocacy, si na session ingine. Kujua ni ipi kati ya mbili problem inahitaji ndio skill yote ya hii module.

🎓 Facilitator note · 12 min
Pick one real problem in the group's own community. Ask two questions: which stage is most people stuck at, and is this an SBCC problem or an advocacy problem? Those two answers determine everything else, and groups almost always get the second one wrong at first.Chagua tatizo moja halisi katika jamii ya kundi. Uliza maswali mawili: watu wengi wamekwama hatua ipi, na je hili ni tatizo la SBCC au la utetezi? Majibu hayo mawili huamua kila kitu kingine, na makundi karibu daima hukosea la pili mwanzoni.Chagua problem moja real kwa community ya group. Uliza questions mbili: wasee wengi wame-stuck stage ipi, na je hii ni SBCC problem ama advocacy problem? Hizo answers mbili zina-determine kila kitu kingine, na groups karibu daima zina-get ya pili wrong mwanzoni.

🔑 Key MessagesUjumbe MuhimuKey Messages
  • Social behaviour change is a strategy for enabling young people to make healthy choices.Mabadiliko ya tabia za kijamii ni mkakati wa kuwawezesha vijana kufanya maamuzi bora ya afya.Social behaviour change ni strategy ya ku-enable vijana ku-make healthy choices.
  • Advocacy targets those who decide; SBCC targets the community. Know which a problem needs.Utetezi hulenga wanaoamua; SBCC hulenga jamii. Jua tatizo linahitaji lipi.Advocacy ina-target wanao-decide; SBCC ina-target community. Jua problem inahitaji ipi.
  • Behaviour changes through eight stages, and people get stuck at different ones.Tabia hubadilika kupitia hatua nane, na watu hukwama katika hatua tofauti.Behaviour ina-change kupitia stages nane, na wasee wana-stuck kwa stages tofauti.

  • Mass media moves awareness. Interpersonal communication moves decisions — that is your work.Vyombo vya habari husogeza uelewa. Mawasiliano ya ana kwa ana husogeza maamuzi — hiyo ni kazi yako.Mass media ina-move awareness. Interpersonal communication ina-move decisions — hiyo ni kazi yako.
