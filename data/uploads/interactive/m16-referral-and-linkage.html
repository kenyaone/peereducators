📖 Two Words, Two Different JobsManeno Mawili, Kazi Mbili TofautiManeno Mbili, Jobs Mbili Tofauti

Most people use these two words as if they mean the same thing. They do not, and the difference is where young people get lost.Watu wengi hutumia maneno haya mawili kana kwamba yana maana moja. Hayana, na tofauti hiyo ndipo vijana hupotea.Wasee wengi wanatumia hizi maneno mbili kama zina maana moja. Hazina, na hiyo tofauti ndio mahali vijana wanapotea.

🔑 Referral🔑 Rufaa🔑 ReferralA deliberate hand-off that connects a 10–24 year-old to a service they need — HIV testing, PrEP, PEP, family planning, GBV care, mental health counselling, social protection or legal support.Kukabidhi kwa makusudi kunakomuunganisha kijana wa miaka 10–24 na huduma anayohitaji — kipimo cha HIV, PrEP, PEP, uzazi wa mpango, huduma za ukatili wa kijinsia, ushauri wa afya ya akili, ulinzi wa kijamii au msaada wa kisheria.Deliberate hand-off inayo-connect msee wa 10–24 na service anayohitaji — HIV testing, PrEP, PEP, family planning, GBV care, mental health counselling, social protection ama legal support.
🔑 Linkage🔑 Uunganishaji🔑 LinkageThe processes and partnerships that make sure the young person actually reaches, receives and continues that care — with feedback coming back to you.Michakato na ushirikiano unaohakikisha kijana anafika, anapokea na anaendelea na huduma hiyo — na maoni yakirudi kwako.Processes na partnerships zinazohakikisha kijana ana-reach, ana-receive na anaendelea na hiyo care — na feedback inarudi kwako.
⚠️ The distinction that matters⚠️ Tofauti ya maana⚠️ Tofauti ya maana A phone number handed over is not a referral. A referral you never followed up is not linkage. If the young person did not receive the service, the job is not finished — whatever you wrote down. Nambari ya simu uliyompa si rufaa. Rufaa usiyoifuatilia si uunganishaji. Kama kijana hakupokea huduma, kazi haijaisha — hata uliandika nini. Number uliyompatia si referral. Referral ambayo hukui-follow up si linkage. Kama kijana hakupata service, job haijaisha — hata uliandika nini.
🪜 The Three StepsHatua TatuSteps Tatu

You identify the need, give first-line information, do a brief risk assessment — and then these three steps follow, in order.Unatambua hitaji, unatoa taarifa za awali, unafanya tathmini fupi ya hatari — kisha hatua hizi tatu zinafuata, kwa mpangilio.Una-identify need, unatoa first-line info, unafanya brief risk assessment — kisha hizi steps tatu zinafuata, kwa order.

  1. Issue and record the referral. Write it down. An undocumented referral cannot be followed up, and cannot be counted.Toa na rekodi rufaa. Iandike. Rufaa isiyoandikwa haiwezi kufuatiliwa, wala kuhesabiwa.Issue na record referral. Iandike. Referral isiyo-recorded haiwezi ku-followiwa up, wala kuhesabiwa.
  2. Follow up until the service is received. Not until they leave you — until they arrive there.Fuatilia hadi huduma ipokewe. Si hadi wakuache — hadi wafike kule.Follow up mpaka service i-receiviwe. Si mpaka wakuache — mpaka wafike kule.
  3. Re-refer or escalate when needed. If it did not land, it is not their failure. Send it again, or send it higher.Toa rufaa tena au peleka juu inapohitajika. Kama haikufika, si kosa lao. Ituma tena, au ipeleke juu.Re-refer ama escalate inapohitajika. Kama haiku-land, si failure yao. Ituma tena, ama ipeleke juu.

Karibu kila jibu ni 'msee alirudi kucheki' — ambayo ndio hasa step 2.

✅ What a Good Referral System Looks LikeMfumo Mzuri wa Rufaa UnavyoonekanaReferral System Poa Inavyoonekana

  • Youth-friendly — after-school and weekend hours, private space, fast-track for referred young peopleRafiki kwa vijana — saa za baada ya shule na wikendi, nafasi ya faragha, njia ya haraka kwa waliopewa rufaaYouth-friendly — masaa ya after-school na weekend, private space, fast-track kwa waliopewa referral
  • Close and reachable — near where young people live, study or workKaribu na unaofikika — karibu na wanapoishi, kusoma au kufanya kaziKaribu na inayo-reachika — karibu na wanapoishi, kusoma ama kufanya kazi

⚖️ The Young Person's Rights in a ReferralHaki za Kijana Katika RufaaRights za Kijana Kwa Referral

You are handing someone into a system. These are the things they are entitled to once they get there — and the things you should tell them to expect.Unamkabidhi mtu kwenye mfumo. Haya ndiyo mambo anayostahili akifika huko — na mambo unayopaswa kumwambia atarajie.Unamkabidhi msee kwa system. Hizi ndio vitu anastahili akifika kule — na vitu unafaa kumwambia a-expect.

  • Privacy comes first — the service should be friendly and should not judge themFaragha kwanza — huduma iwe rafiki na isihukumuPrivacy kwanza — service iwe friendly na isijudge
  • Consent and assent matter — under 18, some services need a parent or guardian, unless the young person is considered mature or emancipatedRidhaa ni muhimu — chini ya miaka 18, huduma zingine zinahitaji mzazi au mlezi, isipokuwa kijana anachukuliwa kuwa amekomaa au huruConsent na assent ni muhimu — chini ya 18, services zingine zinahitaji mzazi ama guardian, unless kijana anachukuliwa mature ama emancipated
  • No stigma, no harm — nobody should make them feel ashamed for comingHakuna unyanyapaa — hakuna anayepaswa kuwafanya waaibike kwa kujaNo stigma, no harm — hakuna anayefaa kuwafanya waskie aibu kwa kuja
  • Follow-up is part of the service — not just a paper, but making sure they get the helpUfuatiliaji ni sehemu ya huduma — si karatasi tu, bali kuhakikisha wanapata msaadaFollow-up ni part ya service — si karatasi tu, ni kuhakikisha wanapata msaada
  • Digital reminders only with consent — an SMS or WhatsApp can expose someone at homeVikumbusho vya kidijitali kwa ridhaa tu — SMS au WhatsApp inaweza kumweka wazi mtu nyumbaniDigital reminders na consent tu — SMS ama WhatsApp inaweza ku-expose msee nyumbani
  • Extra support for those who need it — young mothers, adolescents with disabilities, those out of schoolMsaada wa ziada kwa wanaohitaji — mama vijana, vijana wenye ulemavu, walio nje ya shuleExtra support kwa wanaohitaji — young mothers, vijana wenye disability, walio nje ya shule

🔑 Key MessagesUjumbe MuhimuKey Messages
  • Referral and linkage must always protect privacy and confidentiality.Rufaa na uunganishaji lazima daima vilinde faragha na usiri.Referral na linkage lazima daima zilinde privacy na confidentiality.
  • A referral is a deliberate hand-off — not a phone number handed over.Rufaa ni kukabidhi kwa makusudi — si nambari ya simu iliyotolewa.Referral ni deliberate hand-off — si number iliyotolewa.
  • Linkage means following up until the service is actually received.Uunganishaji maana yake ni kufuatilia hadi huduma ipokewe kweli.Linkage ina maana ku-follow up mpaka service i-receiviwe kweli.
  • Record every referral — an undocumented referral cannot be followed up.Rekodi kila rufaa — rufaa isiyoandikwa haiwezi kufuatiliwa.Record kila referral — referral isiyo-recorded haiwezi ku-followiwa up.
  • Re-refer or escalate when the first attempt does not land.Toa rufaa tena au peleka juu jaribio la kwanza linaposhindwa.Re-refer ama escalate wakati attempt ya kwanza hai-land.
