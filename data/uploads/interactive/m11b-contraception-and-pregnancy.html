🔑 Key MessagesUjumbe MuhimuKey Messages
  • Adolescents face real risks — teenage pregnancy, unsafe abortion, STIs including HIV, and maternal death.Vijana wanakabiliwa na hatari halisi — mimba za utotoni, utoaji mimba usio salama, magonjwa ya zinaa ikiwemo VVU, na vifo vya uzazi.Vijana wanaface real risks — teenage pregnancy, unsafe abortion, STIs ikiwemo HIV, na maternal death.
  • Teenage pregnancy is driven by poverty, low education, peer pressure, sexual violence, drug use and poor access to services.Mimba za utotoni husababishwa na umaskini, elimu duni, shinikizo la rika, ukatili wa kingono, matumizi ya dawa na huduma duni.Teenage pregnancy ina-driviwa na poverty, low education, peer pressure, sexual violence, drug use na poor access ya services.
  • Contraceptives prevent unplanned pregnancy — but only barrier methods also prevent STIs.Uzazi wa mpango huzuia mimba zisizopangwa — lakini njia za kizuizi peke yake ndizo pia huzuia magonjwa ya zinaa.Contraceptives zina-prevent unplanned pregnancy — lakini barrier methods peke yake ndio pia zina-prevent STIs.
  • Myths, stigma and fear stop young people using contraception — which is why correct information and youth-friendly services matter.Imani za uongo, unyanyapaa na hofu huwazuia vijana kutumia uzazi wa mpango — ndiyo maana taarifa sahihi na huduma rafiki ni muhimu.Myths, stigma na fear zinawazuia vijana kutumia contraception — ndio maana correct information na youth-friendly services ni muhimu.
  • Unsafe abortion is a leading cause of adolescent maternal death, and stigma is one of its main drivers.Utoaji mimba usio salama ni sababu kuu ya vifo vya uzazi kwa vijana, na unyanyapaa ni mojawapo ya vichocheo vyake vikuu.Unsafe abortion ni leading cause ya adolescent maternal death, na stigma ni mojawapo ya main drivers zake.
