💡 Why the alternative matters in the conversation💡 Kwa nini mbadala ni muhimu kwenye mazungumzo💡 Kwa nini alternative ni muhimu kwa conversation Families who practise FGM are usually trying to mark something real — that a girl has become a woman, and belongs. Attacking the belonging wins nothing. Offering a way to mark it that does not cut is what actually shifts communities. Familia zinazofanya ukeketaji kwa kawaida hujaribu kuadhimisha jambo halisi — kwamba msichana amekuwa mwanamke, na ni wa jamii. Kushambulia hali hiyo hakushindi chochote. Kutoa njia ya kuadhimisha isiyokata ndiyo inayobadilisha jamii kweli. Families zinazofanya FGM kwa kawaida zina-try ku-mark kitu real — ati dem amekuwa mwanamke, na ni wa community. Ku-attack hiyo belonging haishindi kitu. Kutoa njia ya ku-mark isiyokata ndio ina-shift communities kweli.
