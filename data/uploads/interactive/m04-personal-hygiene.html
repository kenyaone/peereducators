💡 Puberty changes the requirement💡 Ubalehe hubadilisha hitaji💡 Puberty inabadilisha requirement Sweat glands become active, skin becomes oilier, and body odour appears where it did not exist before. A young person is not being careless — their body genuinely changed, and nobody told them the routine had to change with it. Say that plainly; it removes a lot of embarrassment. Tezi za jasho huanza kufanya kazi, ngozi huwa na mafuta zaidi, na harufu ya mwili hujitokeza pasipokuwepo awali. Kijana si mzembe — mwili wake ulibadilika kweli, na hakuna aliyemwambia kwamba utaratibu nao ulipaswa kubadilika. Sema hivyo wazi; kunaondoa aibu nyingi. Sweat glands zinaanza kufanya kazi, ngozi inakuwa na mafuta zaidi, na body odour inajitokeza pasipokuwepo awali. Kijana si careless — mwili wake uli-change kweli, na hakuna aliyemwambia ati routine nayo ilifaa ku-change. Sema hivyo wazi; inaondoa aibu nyingi.

⚠️ Water alone is not handwashing⚠️ Maji peke yake si kunawa mikono⚠️ Maji peke yake si handwashing Rinsing with water shifts visible dirt and leaves most germs in place. Soap is what lifts them off the skin so the water can carry them away. If soap is genuinely unavailable, ash works — but water alone does not. Kusuuza kwa maji huondoa uchafu unaoonekana na huacha vijidudu vingi mahali pake. Sabuni ndiyo inayoviondoa kwenye ngozi ili maji yaviondoe. Sabuni ndio inayo-lift kutoka ngozi ili maji yaziondoe. Kama sabuni haipatikani kabisa, jivu inawork — lakini maji peke yake hai-work.

💡 The answer to the Big Question💡 Jibu la Swali Kubwa💡 Jibu la Big Question Handwashing fails not from ignorance but from friction — no soap at the latrine, no water point near the kitchen, no habit attached to the moment. Fixing the place is more effective than repeating the message. Ask your group where the soap actually is in their school. Kunawa mikono hushindwa si kwa ujinga bali kwa ugumu — hakuna sabuni chooni, hakuna maji karibu na jikoni, hakuna mazoea yaliyoshikamana na wakati huo. Kurekebisha mahali kuna ufanisi zaidi kuliko kurudia ujumbe. Uliza kundi lako sabuni iko wapi hasa shuleni kwao. Handwashing ina-fail si kwa ignorance ni kwa friction — hakuna sabuni chooni, hakuna water point karibu na jikoni, hakuna habit iliyo-attachiwa kwa huo moment. Ku-fix mahali ina effectiveness zaidi kuliko kurudia message. Uliza group yako sabuni iko wapi hasa shuleni kwao.
