💡 The I-statement💡 Sentensi ya 'Mimi'💡 I-statement 'I feel hurt when you ignore me' works where 'you always ignore me' does not. The first states a feeling and invites a response; the second states a verdict and invites a fight. Teach the swap — it is the single most portable communication tool in this module. 'Nahisi kuumizwa unaponipuuza' hufanya kazi pale 'wewe daima hunipuuza' isipofanya. Ya kwanza inaeleza hisia na kualika jibu; ya pili inaeleza hukumu na kualika ugomvi. Fundisha ubadilishaji huo — ni kifaa cha mawasiliano kinachohamishika zaidi katika moduli hii. 'Nahisi kuumizwa unaponi-ignore' inawork pale 'wewe daima huni-ignore' isipowork. Ya kwanza ina-state feeling na ku-invite response; ya pili ina-state verdict na ku-invite fight. Fundisha hiyo swap — ni tool ya communication inayo-portable zaidi kwa hii module.

Inaweza kuwa positive ama negative.

Your job is not to remove peer influence — it is to become a source of the useful kind. Shinikizo la rika ndio mbinu ambayo elimu ya rika yenyewe hufanya kazi kwayo. 🎓 Facilitator note · 12 min
Give the group one real dilemma — a friend asking them to lie to a parent, say — and walk it through all six decision-making steps out loud. The value is in step 3 and step 5, which almost nobody does naturally: exploring consequences before deciding, and asking who else this lands on.Lipe kundi tatizo moja halisi — rafiki akiwaomba wamdanganye mzazi, kwa mfano — na lipitishe kwenye hatua zote sita za kufanya maamuzi kwa sauti.
