💡 Why the social column matters to you💡 Kwa nini safu ya kijamii ni muhimu kwako💡 Kwa nini social column ni muhimu kwako The physical and emotional harm is done by the perpetrator. The social harm — stigma, rejection, isolation — is done by the community afterwards. That second layer is the one a peer educator is actually positioned to prevent. Madhara ya kimwili na kihisia hufanywa na mtendaji. Madhara ya kijamii — unyanyapaa, kukataliwa, kutengwa — hufanywa na jamii baadaye. Social harm — stigma, rejection, isolation — inafanywa na community baadaye. Hiyo layer ya pili ndio peer educator yuko kwa position ya kui-prevent.

🔑 Key MessagesUjumbe MuhimuKey Messages
  • GBV causes serious, lasting harm — physical, emotional and social.Ukatili wa kijinsia husababisha madhara makubwa ya kudumu — ya kimwili, kihisia na kijamii.GBV inasababisha serious, lasting harm — physical, emotional na social.
  • It can be done by anyone: relatives, friends, teachers, partners or strangers.Unaweza kufanywa na yeyote: ndugu, marafiki, walimu, wapenzi au wageni.Inaweza kufanywa na yeyote: relatives, friends, teachers, partners ama strangers.
  • A child is anyone under 18, and consent is never a defence in defilement.Mtoto ni yeyote chini ya miaka 18, na ridhaa si utetezi kamwe katika kudhalilisha mtoto.Mtoto ni yeyote chini ya 18, na consent si defence kamwe kwa defilement.
  • Responsibility always lies with the person who chose to cause harm — never with the survivor.Lawama daima ni ya aliyechagua kudhuru — kamwe si ya manusura.Responsibility daima ni ya aliye-choose kudhuru — kamwe si ya survivor.
