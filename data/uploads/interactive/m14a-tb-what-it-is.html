🔑 Tuberculosis (TB)🔑 Kifua Kikuu (TB)🔑 Tuberculosis (TB)An infectious disease that spreads from person to person through the air, caused by a bacterium called Mycobacterium tuberculosis.Ugonjwa wa kuambukiza unaoenea kutoka mtu hadi mtu kupitia hewa, unaosababishwa na bakteria aitwaye Mycobacterium tuberculosis.Infectious disease inayospread kutoka msee hadi msee kupitia hewa, inayosababishwa na bacteria anayeitwa Mycobacterium tuberculosis.
  • TB germs spread in air droplets when an infected person coughs, breathes, talks, sings or sneezesVijidudu vya TB huenea katika matone ya hewa mtu aliyeambukizwa anapokohoa, kupumua, kuzungumza, kuimba au kupiga chafyaTB germs zinaspread kwa air droplets msee aliyeambukizwa anapokohoa, kupumua, kuongea, kuimba ama kupiga chafya
  • It mainly affects the lungs, but can attack almost any living tissue in the body — except hair, teeth and nailsHuathiri hasa mapafu, lakini kinaweza kushambulia karibu tishu yoyote hai mwilini — isipokuwa nywele, meno na kuchaInaathiri hasa lungs, lakini inaweza ku-attack karibu tissue yoyote hai mwilini — isipokuwa nywele, meno na kucha
  • It is one of the top ten causes of death worldwide — and it is completely preventable and treatableNi kati ya sababu kumi kuu za vifo duniani — na kinaweza kuzuiwa na kutibiwa kabisaNi kati ya top ten causes za vifo duniani — na inaweza ku-preventiwa na ku-treatiwa kabisa
  • TB remains a leading health challenge in high-burden settings like KenyaKifua kikuu bado ni changamoto kuu ya afya katika maeneo yenye mzigo mkubwa kama KenyaTB bado ni leading health challenge kwa high-burden settings kama Kenya
⚠️ Airborne, not contact⚠️ Kwa hewa, si kwa kugusana⚠️ Airborne, si contact TB does not spread through touch, food, sharing plates or hugs. It spreads through air. Get this precise, because the wrong version of it is what makes people isolate someone who is already frightened and unwell. Kifua kikuu hakiambukizwi kwa kugusana, chakula, kushiriki sahani au kukumbatiana. Huenea kwa hewa. Fahamu hili kwa usahihi, kwa sababu toleo lisilo sahihi ndilo linalowafanya watu wamtenge aliyekwisha ogopa na kuugua. TB haiambukizwi kwa touch, chakula, kushare plates ama hugs. Inaspread kupitia hewa. Jua hii precisely, kwa sababu wrong version ndio inafanya wasee wam-isolate msee aliyeshaogopa na kuugua.

💡 How TB is confirmed💡 Jinsi kifua kikuu kinavyothibitishwa💡 Jinsi TB ina-confirmiwa TB is confirmed in the laboratory by testing sputum — kikohozi. A peer educator does not diagnose TB and cannot tell from symptoms alone. What you can do is recognise the pattern and get the person to a facility where the test happens. Kifua kikuu huthibitishwa maabara kwa kupima makohozi — kikohozi. Mwelimishaji rika hatambui kifua kikuu wala hawezi kujua kwa dalili peke yake. Unachoweza kufanya ni kutambua mchoro na kumfikisha mtu kituoni ambapo kipimo hufanyika. TB ina-confirmiwa lab kwa ku-test sputum — kikohozi. Peer educator ha-diagnose TB wala hawezi kujua kwa symptoms peke yake. Unachoweza kufanya ni ku-recognise pattern na kumfikisha msee facility ambapo test inahappen.

⚠️ Why Young People Are at RiskKwa Nini Vijana Wako HatariniKwa Nini Vijana Wako at Risk

Young people are at higher risk for a reason that has nothing to do with their behaviour: they spend most of their lives in crowded shared spaces — schools, clubs, dormitories, social gatherings. TB travels in shared air.Vijana wako katika hatari zaidi kwa sababu isiyohusiana na tabia zao: hutumia sehemu kubwa ya maisha yao katika maeneo yenye msongamano — shule, vilabu, mabweni, mikusanyiko. Kifua kikuu husafiri katika hewa inayoshirikiwa.Vijana wako kwa higher risk kwa sababu isiyohusiana na behaviour yao: wanatumia sehemu kubwa ya maisha yao kwa crowded shared spaces — shule, clubs, dorms, gatherings. TB inasafiri kwa shared air.
