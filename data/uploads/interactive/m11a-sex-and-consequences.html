🧭 Deciding, Rather Than DriftingKuamua, Badala ya KuyumbaKu-decide, Badala ya Ku-drift

Most young people who regret a sexual decision did not decide anything — the situation decided for them. These four steps, from Module 13A, are how a decision gets made rather than arrived at.Vijana wengi wanaojutia uamuzi wa kingono hawakuamua chochote — hali iliwaamulia. Hatua hizi nne, kutoka Moduli 13A, ndizo jinsi uamuzi unavyofanywa badala ya kufikiwa.Vijana wengi wanao-regret sexual decision hawaku-decide kitu — situation iliwa-decide. Hizi steps nne, kutoka Module 13A, ndio jinsi decision inavyofanywa badala ya ku-arriviwa.

  1. Stop and think — what could actually happen here?Simama na ufikiri — ni nini kinaweza kutokea hapa hasa?Stop na ufikiri — ni nini kinaweza ku-happen hapa hasa?
  2. Get the facts — know the risks and the preventionPata ukweli — jua hatari na kingaPata facts — jua risks na prevention
  3. Check your values — does this align with what is right for you?Angalia maadili yako — je, hii inaendana na kilicho sahihi kwako?Check values zako — je, hii ina-align na kilicho sahihi kwako?
  4. Choose safety — protect your health now and in the futureChagua usalama — linda afya yako sasa na baadayeChagua safety — linda health yako saa hii na baadaye
🎓 Facilitator note · 12 min
Do not run this as a debate about whether young people should have sex — that argument has no winner and costs you the room. Run it as a decision-quality exercise: take a scenario the group supplies, and walk the four steps aloud. The skill transfers to every other decision in the curriculum.Usiiendeshe kama mjadala wa kama vijana wanapaswa kufanya ngono — hoja hiyo haina mshindi na inakugharimu chumba. Iendeshe kama zoezi la ubora wa uamuzi: chukua hali ambayo kundi limetoa, na upitie hatua nne kwa sauti. Ujuzi huo huhamia kwenye kila uamuzi mwingine katika mtaala.Usii-run kama debate ya kama vijana wanafaa kufanya sex — hiyo argument haina winner na inakugharimu room.
