People living with HIV have rights, and discrimination against them is wrong.Kuishi vyema na VVU maana yake kuchukua ARV kila siku, kula vizuri, kuwa na shughuli, kutunza ustawi wako wa akili, na kuwa na watu karibu nawe — vikundi vya msaada na mitandao ya rika. 💡 Undetectable = Untransmittable💡 Isiyogundulika = Haiambukizi💡 Undetectable = Untransmittable Taking ARVs every day brings the amount of virus in the blood so low it cannot be detected — and at that point it cannot be passed on. This is why treatment is prevention, and why someone on treatment is not a danger to anybody. Kuchukua ARV kila siku hupunguza kiasi cha virusi damuni hadi kisiweze kugundulika — na wakati huo hakiwezi kupitishwa. Ndiyo maana matibabu ni kinga, na ndiyo maana aliye kwenye matibabu si hatari kwa yeyote. Kuchukua ARVs kila siku inapunguza kiasi cha virus kwa damu hadi kisiweze ku-detectiwa — na wakati huo haiwezi ku-passiwa. Ndio maana treatment ni prevention, na ndio maana msee aliye kwa treatment si danger kwa yeyote.

🔑 Key MessagesUjumbe MuhimuKey Messages
  • HIV does not spread through everyday contact.VVU haviambukizwi kwa mguso wa kila siku.HIV haiambukizwi kwa everyday contact.
  • Protect yourself by avoiding risky behaviours and never sharing sharp objects.Jilinde kwa kuepuka tabia hatarishi na kutoshiriki vitu vyenye ncha kali kamwe.Ji-protect kwa ku-avoid risky behaviours na kutoshare sharp objects kamwe.

  • Testing and treatment save lives. You cannot know your status any other way.Kupimwa na matibabu huokoa maisha. Huwezi kujua hali yako kwa njia nyingine.Testing na treatment zinaokoa maisha. Huwezi kujua status yako kwa njia nyingine.
