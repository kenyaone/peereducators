⚠️ The answer to the Big Question⚠️ Jibu la Swali Kubwa⚠️ Jibu la Big Question Every one of these changes arrives at a different age for every person, and each young person only ever sees their own body from the inside while seeing everyone else's from the outside. That asymmetry — not any real abnormality — is what convinces almost every adolescent that something is wrong with them. Saying so out loud is one of the most useful things a peer educator does. Kila moja ya mabadiliko haya hufika kwa umri tofauti kwa kila mtu, na kila kijana huuona mwili wake kutoka ndani tu huku akiwaona wengine kutoka nje. Tofauti hiyo — si kasoro yoyote halisi — ndiyo inayomshawishi karibu kila kijana kwamba kuna kitu kibaya kwake. Kusema hivyo kwa sauti ni kimoja cha vitu vyenye manufaa zaidi anavyofanya mwelimishaji rika. Kila moja ya hizi changes inafika kwa age tofauti kwa kila msee, na kila kijana anauona mwili wake kutoka ndani tu huku akiwaona wengine kutoka nje. Hiyo asymmetry — si abnormality yoyote real — ndio inayo-convince karibu kila kijana ati kuna kitu kibaya kwake. Kusema hivyo kwa sauti ni kimoja cha vitu vyenye manufaa zaidi anavyofanya peer educator.

🔑 Key MessagesUjumbe MuhimuKey Messages
  • Adolescence is the transition from childhood to adulthood, ages 10 to 19, with physical, emotional and social change.Ujana ni mpito kutoka utotoni hadi utu uzima, miaka 10 hadi 19, wenye mabadiliko ya kimwili, kihisia na kijamii.Adolescence ni transition kutoka utotoni hadi utu uzima, miaka 10 hadi 19, na physical, emotional na social change.
  • Physical changes include growth spurts, body shape change, hair growth, acne, body odour and sexual maturity — menarche in girls, sperm production and semenarche in boys.Mabadiliko ya kimwili ni pamoja na ukuaji wa haraka, mabadiliko ya umbo, ukuaji wa nywele, chunusi, harufu ya mwili na ukomavu wa kingono — hedhi ya kwanza kwa wasichana, utengenezaji wa mbegu na semenarche kwa wavulana.Physical changes ni pamoja na growth spurts, body shape change, hair growth, acne, body odour na sexual maturity — menarche kwa wasichana, sperm production na semenarche kwa wavulana.
  • Social and emotional changes include mood swings, desire for independence, peer influence, curiosity and questioning authority.Mabadiliko ya kijamii na kihisia ni pamoja na mabadiliko ya hisia, hamu ya kujitegemea, ushawishi wa rika, udadisi na kuhoji mamlaka.Social na emotional changes ni pamoja na mood swings, hamu ya independence, peer influence, curiosity na ku-question authority.
  • There is no single normal. Every change arrives at a different age for every person.Hakuna ukawaida mmoja. Kila badiliko hufika kwa umri tofauti kwa kila mtu.Hakuna normal moja. Kila change inafika kwa age tofauti kwa kila msee.
  • Good menstrual hygiene means clean pads or cloths, changing regularly, washing daily and safe disposal.Usafi mzuri wa hedhi ni pedi au vitambaa safi, kubadilisha mara kwa mara, kuosha kila siku na kutupa kwa usalama.Good menstrual hygiene ni clean pads ama cloths, kubadilisha regularly, kuosha kila siku na safe disposal.
