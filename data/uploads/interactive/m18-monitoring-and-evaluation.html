⚠️ The answer to the Big Question⚠️ Jibu la Swali Kubwa⚠️ Jibu la Big Question A thousand reached is a monitoring figure — it counts activity. Whether anything changed is an evaluation question, and it was never asked. A programme that only monitors can report impressive numbers forever while achieving nothing, and nobody in it will know. Elfu moja waliofikiwa ni takwimu ya ufuatiliaji — inahesabu shughuli. Kama kuna kilichobadilika ni swali la tathmini, na halikuulizwa kamwe. Programu inayofuatilia tu inaweza kuripoti namba za kuvutia milele huku haifanikishi chochote, na hakuna aliye ndani yake atakayejua. Elfu moja walio-reachiwa ni figure ya monitoring — ina-count activity. Kama kuna kilicho-change ni question ya evaluation, na haiku-askiwa kamwe. Programme inayo-monitor tu inaweza ku-report numbers za kuvutia forever huku haifanikishi kitu, na hakuna msee ndani yake atakayejua.

⚠️ Bad data is worse than no data⚠️ Data mbaya ni mbaya kuliko kutokuwa na data⚠️ Bad data ni mbaya kuliko kutokuwa na data Missing data tells you that you do not know. Wrong data tells you something false with confidence — and decisions get made on it. Rounding up attendance, filling a register at month-end from memory, or recording a referral you never followed up all produce a programme that looks like it is working while it is not. Data inayokosekana inakuambia kwamba hujui. Data isiyo sahihi inakuambia jambo la uongo kwa kujiamini — na maamuzi hufanywa juu yake. Kuongeza mahudhurio, kujaza rejista mwisho wa mwezi kwa kumbukumbu, au kurekodi rufaa usiyoifuatilia yote hutoa programu inayoonekana inafanya kazi ilhali haifanyi. Missing data inakuambia ati hujui. Wrong data inakuambia kitu cha uongo kwa confidence — na decisions zina-madiwa juu yake. Ku-round up attendance, kujaza register month-end kwa memory, ama ku-record referral usiyoi-follow up zote zinatoa programme inayoonekana ina-work ilhali hai-work.

🔑 Key MessagesUjumbe MuhimuKey Messages
  • Monitoring watches what is happening; evaluation asks whether it helped.Ufuatiliaji huangalia kinachoendelea; tathmini huuliza kama kulisaidia.Monitoring ina-watch kinachohappen; evaluation inauliza kama kulisaidia.
  • Data recorded badly is worse than no data — it misleads with confidence.Data iliyorekodiwa vibaya ni mbaya kuliko kutokuwa na data — hupotosha kwa kujiamini.Data iliyo-recordiwa vibaya ni mbaya kuliko kutokuwa na data — inapotosha kwa confidence.
  • Record at the time, never from memory at month-end, and never record names on sensitive topics.Rekodi wakati huo, kamwe si kwa kumbukumbu mwisho wa mwezi, na usirekodi majina kwenye mada nyeti.Record wakati huo, kamwe si kwa memory month-end, na usi-record majina kwa sensitive topics.
  • M&E exists to improve the programme, not only to satisfy donors.M&E ipo kuboresha programu, si kuridhisha wafadhili peke yake.M&E iko ku-improve programme, si ku-satisfy donors peke yake.
  • Young people's voices belong in the evaluation, not just in the numbers. Nothing for us, without us.Sauti za vijana zinastahili kuwa katika tathmini, si kwenye namba peke yake. Hakuna kwa ajili yetu, bila sisi.Voices za vijana zinafaa kuwa kwa evaluation, si kwa numbers peke yake. Nothing for us, without us.
